# Blood Donation Web App

A community-centered application designed to improve how hospitals, blood banks, and individual donors coordinate to meet urgent and routine blood needs. This README describes what the project is about from the perspective of the three main user sides — Hospital, User (Donor), and Blood Bank — without any technical implementation details.

## Overview

The goal of this project is to make blood availability faster, more reliable, and easier to manage by improving communication and coordination between people who need blood, volunteers who donate, and organizations that allocate supplies. It focuses on clarity, speed, and safety so patients receive the blood they need when they need it.

## Roles

- Hospital
  - Publish requests when a patient needs blood, indicating blood group, number of units, urgency, and any special notes.
  - Track responses and confirmations from donors and blood banks.
  - Coordinate with blood banks to ensure timely fulfillment and arrange transfusion logistics.

- User (Donor)
  - Sign up as a voluntary donor and keep basic profile information (e.g., blood group and donation eligibility).
  - See nearby or urgent requests that match their blood type and availability.
  - Respond to requests they can fulfill and receive appointment or donation instructions.
  - Get notified about emergency appeals, scheduled donation opportunities, and confirmation updates.

- Blood Bank
  - Maintain and update inventory levels by blood type and make supplies visible to hospitals.
  - Accept and fulfill hospital requests by allocating available units.
  - Coordinate donor appointments and manage collection and testing logistics.
  - Track dispatch and delivery to hospitals and confirm completed transfers.

## Key Non-Technical Features

- Hospital request creation and publication for patients in need
- Clear searching and matching so donors see requests that suit their blood type and location
- Inventory visibility for blood banks to manage supplies and fulfill requests
- Notifications and confirmations for donors, hospitals, and blood banks throughout the request lifecycle
- Appointment scheduling to make donation predictable and reduce no-shows
- Emphasis on donor and patient privacy and responsible handling of personal information

## Benefits

- Faster response during emergencies through direct, coordinated communication
- Better inventory management to reduce shortages and wastage
- Easier participation for willing donors with straightforward alerts and scheduling
- Stronger collaboration between hospitals and blood banks to improve patient care

## How It Works (High-Level)

1. Choose your role: Hospital, Donor, or Blood Bank.
2. Hospitals publish a blood request when a patient needs blood.
3. Donors receive alerts or search for requests and respond if they can help.
4. Blood banks confirm availability, coordinate collection, and fulfill hospital requests.
5. All parties receive updates until the request is completed.
